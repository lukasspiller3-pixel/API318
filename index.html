What this helps you notice

The mean arrival and service times don’t tell the whole story. A line can form because a customer arrives during a long service, even when average capacity is higher than average demand.

I learned that queueing depends on more than average arrival and service rates. When I kept the averages at 5 arrivals per hour and 6 services per hour, the more regular arrival pattern and variable service times still produced waits. Comparing the simulation with the exponential-time formula showed me why I need to understand variability and assumptions before using a queueing estimate.
